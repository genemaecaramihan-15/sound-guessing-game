How to Play Guess the Sound

1

🔊 Listen Carefully

Click the large Play Sound button or press Space to hear the mystery audio clip. Listen closely before making your choice!

2

🤔 Pick the Correct Answer

Choose from the 4 options shown on screen. Only one is correct! You can click or press number keys 1 to 4.

3

🌟 Earn Points & Learn

Score 10 points for every correct answer. Read fascinating educational facts about what produces each sound!

4

💀 Beware of Game Over!

Listen closely before choosing! If you choose an incorrect answer, it's Game Over immediately. Try to maintain the longest streak!

5

🌟 Surprise Golden Sound Challenge!

A surprise Golden Sound appears randomly during the game! Guessing correctly awards +30 Bonus Points, an 🛡️ Extra Life Shield (saves you from 1 Game Over!), and the 🌟 Golden Ear Badge!
